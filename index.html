The Building Blocks of Web3

Explore the technologies that form the foundation of the decentralized web.

⛓️ Blockchain

A blockchain is a distributed digital ledger that records transactions across a network without relying on one central authority.

💰 Cryptocurrency

Digital assets that use cryptography and blockchain networks to enable transactions without traditional intermediaries.

🤖 Smart Contracts

Programs stored on a blockchain that automatically execute predefined rules when certain conditions are met.

🎨 NFTs

Non-Fungible Tokens are unique digital tokens that can represent ownership of digital or physical assets.

🏛️ DAOs

Decentralized Autonomous Organizations use blockchain-based rules and governance to coordinate communities.

👛 Web3 Wallets

Digital wallets allow users to manage blockchain accounts, digital assets and interact with decentralized applications.
